Top Road Test Tips to Pass

  • Full Stop at Signs: Come to a complete stop behind the white limit line for 3 full seconds. Never roll through!
  • Exaggerate Shoulder Checks: Visually turn your head over your shoulder to check blind spots before changing lanes or pulling out.
  • Speed Control: Stay strictly within the speed limit. In school zones or residential streets, drive according to conditions.
  • Two Hands on the Wheel: Keep hands in the 9-and-3 or 8-and-4 position throughout the test.
  • Signal 100 Feet Early: Activate turn signals at least 100 feet before making turns or pulling to the curb.
